The Qur'an at a glance

The sacred scripture of Islam, understood by Muslims as revelation received by the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

The 6,236 verse count follows the common Kufan numbering tradition; other verse divisions differ slightly.
Sources and counting notes are listed below.

About the counters: These numbers animate for learning; they do not measure live activity. The worldwide Muslim population is not a count of people currently reading the Qur'an. No reliable worldwide total of Qur'an copies printed annually was found, so publishing data are attributed to a named printing complex.
